Questions

Briefly explained

Does NextToMaps replace Google Maps?

No, it complements it. NextToMaps finds the right place; the navigation there is handled by Google Maps with one tap on “Route”.

What is a Google query?

Each results page we fetch from Google counts as one query. A normal search needs 1 to 3. Conditions such as playground, car park or charging station come from our own database and cost nothing.

Where does it work?

The Google search works everywhere. Our fast database for conditions covers Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Italy and is updated every month.

How accurate are the distances?

The distances are straight-line. The walking route may be somewhat longer.

Do I need an app?

No. NextToMaps runs in the browser. On your phone you can add it to your home screen, and then it feels like an app.
