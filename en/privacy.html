2. Overview

NextToMaps is a map search. We only process the data that is necessary for sign-in, search and protection against misuse. There is no advertising, no tracking, no analytics tools from third-party providers and no cookies for advertising or statistical purposes.

3. Accessing the website (hosting)

The website is provided via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you access the site, GitHub technically processes necessary data such as your IP address, the time, the page accessed and your browser identifier, in order to deliver the page and protect it against attacks.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and functional website). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: GitHub’s privacy statement.

4. Account and sign-in

To search, you sign in with your email address. You receive a sign-in code by email; there is no password. New accounts are checked by us before they are activated. For this purpose we receive an email containing your address.

We store:

  • your email address, the time of registration and the time of your last sign-in,
  • the status of your account (pending, activated, rejected) and your plan,
  • the number of your Google queries per day, in order to calculate the quota,
  • technical sign-in logs (including IP address and time), which our service provider creates for security purposes.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the service you want to use) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for the security logs. The data remains stored for as long as your account exists. On request we will delete your account at any time; please write to the address above.

Supabase

Accounts, sign-in and the database are operated by Supabase, Inc. (970 Toa Payoh North #07-04, Singapore 318992). The data is stored in a data centre in Frankfurt am Main, Germany (EU). A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Supabase. Insofar as Supabase processes data outside the EU, this is based on EU Standard Contractual Clauses. More information: Supabase privacy policy.

Email delivery

Sign-in codes and notifications are sent via the mail server of our email provider (IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany, or Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). In the process, your email address and the content of the message are processed.

5. Search

When you search, your search term, the conditions and the map section or the selected place are processed. If you choose “Around me”, your browser asks for permission and then transmits your approximate location to the app. The location is used only for the search and is not stored in your account.

Google Places

For searches with Google data, our server forwards the search request (search term and map area, not your email address or IP address) to the Google Places API. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; processing in the USA is possible. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. More information: Google privacy policy.

OpenStreetMap

For searches with OpenStreetMap and for the surroundings conditions, your browser queries public Overpass servers (including overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee, overpass.kumi.systems and maps.mail.ru). Place names are converted into coordinates via Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation). These servers thereby receive your IP address and the search query. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

6. Map display

By default, the map is displayed with tiles from OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation, St John’s Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom). Your browser loads the tiles directly from their servers and thereby transmits your IP address. For the United Kingdom, there is an adequacy decision of the European Commission.

If you are signed in, the map may instead be displayed with Google Maps. Your browser then loads map data and scripts directly from Google; your IP address and usage data are transmitted to Google.

The map library Leaflet is loaded via the network of unpkg.com (Cloudflare, Inc., USA); your IP address is processed in the process.

The legal basis in each case is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a functioning map display).

7. Storage in your browser

The app stores in your browser’s local storage (localStorage) your sign-in session, search results for a few hours as a temporary cache, and settings such as the map source. This data does not leave your device and serves only the function of the app (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). You can delete it at any time via your browser settings.

8. Visitor statistics

To see how many people use NextToMaps, we count page views with our own simple statistics. For this we only store the page accessed, the day, the domain of the page you came from (e.g. google.com), and whether you use a phone or a computer. If you arrive via a link with a campaign parameter (e.g. ?utm_source=newsletter), we store this parameter instead of the domain.

So that a visitor is counted only once per day, we form a shortened checksum from your IP address, your browser identifier and a random daily key. The IP address itself is not stored. The daily key is deleted on the following day; after that, the checksum can no longer be assigned to anyone. No cookies are set and no data is passed on to third parties; the data is held at Supabase in Frankfurt and is deleted automatically after 13 months.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in anonymous reach measurement). If your browser sends the “Global Privacy Control” signal, we do not count you.

9. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your message and contact details in order to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete the data once it is no longer needed for this purpose and there are no statutory retention obligations.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). An email to j@kalberlah.info is sufficient.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

11. Changes

We will update this policy if the service changes, for example when paid plans are introduced.
